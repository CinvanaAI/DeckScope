# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

That last one is, not coincidentally, exactly what this project asks its panel agents to
do — engage with the reasoning, change your mind when the evidence is better, hold your
position when it isn't, and never capitulate just to be agreeable.

## What that means here

**Welcome.** Ask beginner questions. Someone who has never opened a terminal is a target
user of this project, and their confusion is usually a documentation bug rather than a
personal failing.

**Disagree well.** Say what you think and why. Be specific — "this misses X because Y" is
useful; "this is bad" is not. Take a well-argued correction gracefully.

**Not welcome.** Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome attention,
publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers privately through the repository's contact channel.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, harmful,
or unprofessional.

Maintainers who don't follow this in good faith may face consequences from the rest of the
project's leadership.
