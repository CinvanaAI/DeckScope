--- Slide 2 ---
The problem
Independent pharmacy groups over-order slow-moving stock and stock out of
fast movers in the same week. Their wholesaler's reorder tool optimises for
the wholesaler.

--- Slide 3 ---
Solution
Demand forecasting per store, per SKU, reconciled against dispensing history
rather than against purchase history.

--- Slide 4 ---
Traction
$2.1M ARR across 34 pharmacy groups.
Net revenue retention of 131% among customers past their first renewal.
Logo retention of 96% among customers past their first renewal.

--- Slide 5 ---
Why we win
Retention is the whole story in this category. Our 131% NRR is well above
the benchmarks published for vertical SaaS, and it compounds: we grow inside
an account as they add stores.

Market
Roughly 22,000 independent pharmacy groups in North America. At our current
$62k average contract value that is a $1.4B serviceable market.
