## The organization

Feeding America is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and the nation's largest
hunger-relief network, coordinating food banks across all fifty states.
Donations are tax-deductible, and the organization's Form 990 filings
are public. Our clients ask us each year whether it remains a sound
home for major charitable gifts. This brief summarizes the case as it
is usually presented to donors.

## The financial picture, as commonly presented

Total revenue reached $5.2 billion in fiscal 2023, driven by
record-setting generosity.
Contributions and grants totaled $4.75 billion in fiscal 2023.
The organization held total expenses to $4.93 billion in fiscal 2023
while scaling operations nationwide.
Revenue has more than doubled since fiscal 2019.

## Efficiency

92 cents of every dollar donated goes directly to hunger-relief
programs.
The chief executive officer's total compensation was $1.2 million,
modest for an organization of this scale.

## Impact

The network serves tens of millions of people facing hunger every
year through member food banks and their volunteers.

## Recommendation

We advise clients that this remains a best-in-class charitable
commitment for donors seeking measurable community impact.
