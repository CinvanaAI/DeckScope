## Specific Aims

HaloSense Labs proposes a smartphone-based sensing platform for
continuous vital-sign monitoring using only the device's existing
camera and inertial sensors. Our specific aims are: (1) validate optical
pulse extraction under motion, (2) ship an on-device inference model
under 40MB, (3) complete a 30-participant feasibility study.

## Intellectual Merit

No NSF-funded effort has attempted smartphone-only continuous vital-sign
monitoring without added hardware. The approach is unprecedented in the
published literature: prior systems require wearables, adhesive sensors,
or clinical equipment.

Our team has published 14 peer-reviewed papers on mobile sensing and
optical physiology in the last five years.

## Broader Impacts

The market need is acute: remote patient monitoring reaches fewer than
20% of eligible patients, and hardware cost is the cited barrier in 61%
of declined enrollments.
