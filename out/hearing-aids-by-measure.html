This report has a denominator and no numerator. The total is unusually good - 23.16 million units in 2025, defined precisely as net wholesale units sold by manufacturers to dispensers, published free by EHIMA with a consistent six-year history. The per-company split does not exist in any source reached.

That gap is structural, not a coverage failure. EHIMA is the manufacturers' own trade association, so the body that counts the units is owned by the firms whose relative shares the count would expose. Further searching will not find this number; it is not being withheld by one publisher, it is not being produced.

No commercial channel panel for hearing aid units was found either. That matters for anyone considering buying a report: in handsets, paying buys a panel that genuinely counts units, whereas here the syndicated reports appear to publish an unattributed combined share and no per-company unit figure at all. The money may buy modelling rather than counting.

The three sources giving 23.16 million all trace to one EHIMA release. Treat it as one measurement reported three times, not as three-way corroboration.

EHIMA members are described as the largest manufacturers but membership is not stated to be exhaustive, so 23.16 million is a floor for worldwide volume rather than a measured total.

The US and Germany volumes are a geographic split, not a company split, and do not help complete the share table.
