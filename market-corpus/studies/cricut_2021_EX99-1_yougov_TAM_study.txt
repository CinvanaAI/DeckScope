SOURCE: Cricut, Inc. (CRCT) — Form S-1, filed 2021-02-16
EXHIBIT: EX-99.1 "Consent to Use Report" (YouGov America, Inc.)
URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1828962/000156459021006015/crct-ex991_713.htm
WHY IT MATTERS: the only case found so far where the commissioned market study's
own description is filed as an exhibit, rather than only summarized in the body.

--- METHOD, AS STATED ---
Study: "2020 Cricut TAM Study Background Information", commissioned from YouGov
America, September 2020, multiple countries, n > 1,000 per country, age 18+.
Survey results extrapolated across the general population 18+ in each country.

SAM definition (behavioral screen):
  individuals who "have made at least one creative project in categories
  addressed by our current products in the last 12 months" -> "active creatives"

TAM definition (broader behavioral screen):
  SAM + individuals who "like, buy, used to make or are interested in creating
  personalized, handmade or custom items" -> "potential creatives"

Geography: US + Canada treated as home market; four named primary international
markets (Australia, France, Germany, UK) sized separately.

RESULTS
  SAM  US+CA        > 85 million people
  SAM  intl (4)     > 44 million people
  SAM  total        > 129 million
  TAM  US+CA        > 248 million
  TAM  intl (4)     > 153 million
  TAM  total        ~ 402 million
  Penetration       3.7M users / 85M SAM  => "more than 4%"

NOTE ON HONESTY: the TAM screen ("interested in creating") is extremely loose and
resolves to most of the adult population. The filing states it plainly enough
that a reader can see this, which is the disclosure working as intended.
